Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GROKHEADS INNOVATION LLP ("Grokheads", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use our food ordering website www.grokheads.com, our Android and iOS apps, and our WhatsApp messages (together, the "Service"). The Service lets you browse menus from restaurants on our platform, including Bob's Restaurant, and place orders for delivery or pickup.

We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Mobile number: to send you a one-time login code on WhatsApp, to identify your account and to contact you about your orders.
  • Name: so restaurants know who the order is for.
  • Delivery addresses and map location: the addresses you save, including the point you pick on the map, to deliver your orders and check that you are within our delivery area.
  • Order details: what you ordered, from which restaurant, prices, discounts, delivery or pickup choice, scheduled time and order status.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications in the app, a device token used to send you order updates.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, dishes added to the cart and similar app events, device and browser type, collected through Google Analytics to understand and improve the Service.

We do not collect payment card details. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18; the Service is meant for adults.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and log you in with a WhatsApp code.
  • To take, prepare, deliver and track your orders, and to share the order with the restaurant preparing it.
  • To send you order confirmations and updates on WhatsApp and through app notifications.
  • To keep accounts, invoices and tax records as required by law, including GST.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to fix problems with the Service.
  • To improve our menus and the Service, using usage data in aggregate.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Restaurants on our platform: the restaurant you order from receives your name, phone number, address and order so it can prepare and deliver it.
  • Service providers that run the Service for us: Microsoft Azure (hosting and image storage), MongoDB Atlas (database), Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business messaging), Google Firebase (app notifications), Google Maps (address search and map location) and Google Analytics (usage statistics). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities: when required by law, a court order or a government request.

Some of these providers may store data outside India; they protect it under their own security and data protection commitments.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved addresses while your account is active. Login codes expire after 5 minutes and are deleted automatically. Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require (for example, GST record-keeping rules). When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except records we must keep by law.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or update your personal data, to delete your account and data, or to withdraw your consent. You can also nominate another person to exercise these rights for you. See how to delete your data, or write to us at the contact below. We respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, hashed login codes and short-lived sessions to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a personal data breach as the law requires.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website and app store your cart, your saved addresses and your login on your device so the Service works between visits. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers to measure usage. You can clear this data from your browser or app settings at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
