User Data Deletion

Effective date: 24 October 2026

You can ask GROKHEADS INNOVATION LLP to delete your Grokheads account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This applies to data from our website, our apps and our WhatsApp messages. Grokheads does not use Facebook Login, so there is no Facebook account data to remove; WhatsApp is used only to send you login codes and order messages.

How to request deletion

Option 1: Email
Send an email to bobs.grokheads@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Grokheads data" and include the mobile number registered with your account.

Option 2: WhatsApp
Send "Delete my data" on WhatsApp to +91 96433 10092 from your registered mobile number.

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request with a one-time code sent to your registered number.

What we delete

  • Your account: mobile number and name.
  • Your saved delivery addresses and map locations.
  • Device tokens used for app notifications.
  • Any pending login codes (these also expire automatically after 5 minutes).

What we keep, and why

Order and invoice records are kept for the period required by tax and accounting laws, such as GST record-keeping rules. Where we must keep them, we remove or anonymise your name, phone number and address wherever the law allows. Usage statistics in Google Analytics are not linked to your name or number and are deleted on Google's normal retention schedule.

How long it takes

We confirm your request within 3 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days, then let you know by email or WhatsApp. To clear data stored on your own device (your cart and saved login), log out and clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
